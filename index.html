Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our workout plans.

How soon will I see results?

Your progress will depend on your genetics and previous training experience. You'll likely notice performance gains within the first few months, whereas visible changes in body composition usually require 3–6 months.

Do I need gym equipment?

A pull-up bar and dip bars are the minimum equipment needed for most calisthenics training, and they're available in many outdoor workout parks. If you're a complete beginner, resistance bands can make learning progressions easier by providing assistance. On the other hand, if you want to incorporate weighted calisthenics, investing in a dip belt, weight plates, and a weighted vest is a great option.
