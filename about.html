About Me

Calisthenics Has Been My Life For Over 15 Years

My name is Bruno Žgela and I have dedicated more than 15 years to mastering bodyweight training, movement, strength and athletic development. Through competing, coaching and thousands of hours of practice, I have developed a system that helps people build strength, confidence and a body they can be proud of.

My Journey

I started my calisthenics journey in 2013, as a 13 year old kid inspired by the freedom of street workout and the idea that you can build incredible strength using nothing more than your own body. Coming from a background in sports, including football and wrestling, I developed the discipline, determination, and competitive mindset that continue to guide me today.

Along the way I learned that real progress doesn't come from shortcuts. It comes from consistency, intelligent programming and patience. These are the principles I now teach every client.

Today my goal is simple: help people avoid years of mistakes and achieve results faster with proper guidance.



Throughout my calisthenics journey, I have always been driven by one goal: to continuously push the limits of what the human body can achieve through bodyweight training.
