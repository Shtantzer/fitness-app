About Me

Calisthenics Has Been My Life For Over 15 Years

My name is Bruno Zgela and I have dedicated more than 15 years to mastering bodyweight training, movement, strength and athletic development. Through competing, coaching and thousands of hours of practice, I have developed a system that helps people build strength, confidence and a body they can be proud of.

My Journey

I discovered calisthenics long before it became mainstream. What started as curiosity quickly became an obsession. I spent years learning through trial and error, studying training principles, competing and constantly improving.

Along the way I learned that real progress doesn't come from shortcuts. It comes from consistency, intelligent programming and patience. These are the principles I now teach every client.

Today my goal is simple: help people avoid years of mistakes and achieve results faster with proper guidance.
